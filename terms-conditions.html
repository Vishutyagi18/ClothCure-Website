Our Services

We offer the following garment care services:

  • Dry cleaning for shirts, tops, bottoms, ethnic wear, coats, blazers, jackets, and woolens

  • Standalone steam ironing and pressing

Current prices for every service are published on our Rate List page. Rates may change from time to time; the version shown at the time you book applies to your order.

Booking & Pickup

You can schedule a pickup through our website, by calling or messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 84489 45599, or by visiting our store directly. To avoid delays, please:

  1. Share an accurate pickup address and a working contact number
  2. Choose a pickup window where someone will be available to hand over garments
  3. Let us know at the time of booking about any garments needing special care

We may occasionally need to reschedule a pickup or delivery because of weather, traffic, or operational constraints. Where this happens, we'll make reasonable efforts to inform you in advance.

Pricing, GST & Payment

All services are charged as per our current Rate List. Laundry services are billed per kilogram; dry cleaning and most other services are billed per piece unless stated otherwise. GST is applicable as per government norms and will be reflected on your bill.

If a garment arrives more heavily soiled, damaged, or embellished than typical for its category, we may confirm a revised price with you before processing. Bulk, corporate, and festival-season orders are quoted separately — please call us for these.

Payment is due at the time of delivery unless a separate corporate or credit arrangement has been agreed in writing. We currently accept cash, UPI, and card payments.

Turnaround Time

Standard delivery is 3–4 working days from pickup. Express same-day or next-day delivery is available on request at an additional 50% charge, subject to availability.

Turnaround times may be affected by weather, festival-season volumes, or circumstances beyond our reasonable control. We will try to keep you informed of any significant delay to your order.
