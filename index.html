  1. 01

    Appointment & Pickup

  2. 02

    Tagging

    Each garment is tagged for identification and the correct cleaning process.

  3. 03

    Stain Removal

    Pre-treatment and spotting — every item checked for stains before cleaning.

  4. 04

    Washing / Dry Clean

    Hygiene-first cleaning with fabric-appropriate solvents and detergents.

  5. 05

    Steam Iron

  6. 06

    Quality Check

    Thorough inspection before packing — we note any remaining stains if applicable.

  7. 07

    Packing

    Neat hanger or fold packing so clothes stay fresh in transit.

  8. 08

    Delivery

    Safe, on-time delivery to your doorstep — fresh and ready to wear.

FAQ

Common Questions

How long does dry cleaning take?

Standard dry cleaning takes 2–3 business days. Express same-day service is available for select items — ask when booking.

Do you offer home pickup and delivery?

Yes! Schedule a pickup through our form or by phone. Free delivery on orders above ₹500 within our service area.

Are your cleaning products safe for delicate fabrics?

Absolutely. We use eco-friendly, fabric-safe solvents and adjust our process based on garment labels and fabric type.

What if I'm not satisfied with the result?

Your satisfaction is our priority. Contact us within 48 hours and we'll re-process the item at no extra charge.

Do you clean wedding and designer wear?

Yes — sarees, lehengas, sherwanis, and designer pieces receive specialized care. Pricing varies by fabric and embellishments.
